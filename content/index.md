# Satyagraha Legal Wiki

Welcome to the public legal-reference library of **Satyagraha Law Group**. This wiki is designed for clear navigation across Indian statutes, procedural materials, schedules, appendices, and forms.

## How to use this wiki

- Use the search box to find provisions, orders, forms, and legal terms.
- Follow the internal links between parts of an Act.
- Use the explorer to browse the hierarchy of the collection.
- Check the source legislation and current amendments before relying on any provision.
